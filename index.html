Research Interests

Scientific Interests

My research sits at the intersection of theoretical physics and biophysics, covering soft matter, neural interfaces, and colloidal interactions.

Physics of Colloids and Bio-colloids

Colloids sit at the boundary of soft matter physics and biology, and their interactions drive many biological processes.

Physics of Cell Membranes

Interactions between membrane inclusions, mediated by the membrane itself.

Physics of Neurons and Neural Interfaces

Physical modeling of neurons and of neural interfaces between devices and living tissue.

Casimir Interactions: A Force from Nothing!

Forces from quantum and thermal fluctuations between colloidal particles at soft interfaces.
