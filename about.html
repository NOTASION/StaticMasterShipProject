Academic Biography

The Researcher

Profile Photo

Dr. Ehsan Noruzifar is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Physics at K. N. Toosi University of Technology, affiliated with the Condensed Matter Group. His research focuses on theoretical physics, biophysics, and interdisciplinary science.

He explores the intersection of theoretical physics and biophysics, with a particular focus on soft matter systems, neural interfaces, and colloidal interactions. Over the past years, he has conducted research in areas such as Casimir interactions and interdisciplinary approaches to biophysical problems.

He enjoys mentoring students and collaborating with colleagues on projects that push the boundaries of our understanding in physics and related sciences.
